Emergency Safety Guide

Follow these basic safety instructions.

🚑

Medical Emergency

Call for medical assistance, keep the person safe and follow instructions from emergency responders.

🔥

Fire Safety

Use the nearest safe exit, avoid lifts and move away from smoke and fire.

🛡️

Personal Safety

Move to a safe public location and contact emergency services or trusted campus staff.

📍

Share Your Location

When contacting emergency responders, provide your exact location and a clear description of the situation.
